# VR HEADSET MANAGER

## How To enable ADB Wifi on your Meta Quest Headset

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Your headset must be first in developper mode. If it's not already done, [please follow the official Meta tutorial](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/documentation/native/android/mobile-device-setup/)

### OPTION 1 : Enable by connecting the headset on your computer
  - Start the VR Headset Manager app
  - Connect your headset with USB to your computer
  - In the VR Headset Manager app, press **+**
  - If your headset is detected (and USB has been authorized in the headset), the ADB Wifi debugging will be automatically enabled.
  - Follow the process to managed automatically the VR headset for screen copy.

The app [oculus-wireless-adb](https://github.com/thedroidgeek/oculus-wireless-adb) is available in **_sources\ADB Wireless activator**
